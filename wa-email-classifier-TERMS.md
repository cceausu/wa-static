# WA Classifier add-on — Terms of Service

**Audience:** internal whitearrow.com users only.

**Effective date:** 2026-05-16.

## What the add-on does

The WA Classifier sidebar surfaces the WA email classifier's verdict for
the currently open Gmail message and lets you submit corrections that
the classifier learns from.

## Acceptable use

By using this add-on you agree to:

- Use it only on White Arrow Workspace accounts (`@whitearrow.com`)
- Use corrections in good faith — submit accurate scope choices ("just
  this email" vs "all from this sender" vs "everyone at this domain")
  to keep the classifier's behavior trustworthy for everyone

## No warranty

The add-on is provided as-is for internal operational use. It is not
SLA-backed. Classifier verdicts are recommendations, not authoritative
labels — review before relying on them for downstream decisions.
